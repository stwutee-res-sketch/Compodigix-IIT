Knowledge base

Feedstock library

Typical composition of common wastes. Mix nitrogen-rich "greens" with carbon-rich "browns" to reach a C:N of about 25–30. Measure your own materials for a real project.

tips_and_updatesRules of thumb
  • checkGreens: food waste, vegetable waste, dung, grass — wet and rich in nitrogen.
  • checkBrowns: straw, dry leaves, sawdust, husk — dry and rich in carbon.
  • checkSqueeze test: a fistful should feel like a wrung-out sponge — a few drops, no stream.
  • checkChop to 2–5 cm. Keep out plastic, glass, metal, batteries and medical waste.

Typical values compiled from the CPHEEO Municipal Solid Waste Management Manual (2016), the On-Farm Composting Handbook (NRAES-54, 1992) and Indian studies; dry-matter basis for C and N.

Knowledge base

Standards & rules

Compost sold as a fertiliser in India must meet the Fertiliser Control Order (FCO) 1985, Schedule IV, and be tested by a notified laboratory. Composition is on a dry-weight basis.

ruleFCO specifications (summary)

Summary for planning; always check the latest notification (FCO as amended up to December 2025). City compost and PROM limits as published by fertiliser companies; vermicompost and FOM as notified in Schedule IV.

thermostatProcess standards
  • checkPathogen kill: windrows ≥ 55 °C for 15 days with at least 5 turnings; aerated piles and vessels ≥ 55 °C for 3 days.
  • checkWindrow composting cycle 60–90 days (CPHEEO 2016): thermophilic phase, mesophilic phase, then 30–45 days curing.
  • checkMoisture 50–60 % while active; turn if above 65 % or below 40 %.
  • checkImpervious pad, leachate drain and a roof in the monsoon for plants of a few tonnes per day and more.
gavelRules that apply
  • checkSolid Waste Management Rules, 2016: segregation at source; bulk waste generators must process wet waste on site; authorisation from the State Pollution Control Board for processing facilities.
  • checkFertiliser Control Order, 1985: registration and quality testing before compost is sold as fertiliser.
  • checkCPHEEO Manual on Municipal Solid Waste Management (2016): design guidance for compost plants in towns and cities.
